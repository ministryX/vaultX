Privacy policy

Short version: VaultX does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. There is no VaultX server.

No collection

No analytics, telemetry, crash reports, ads or tracking. No VaultX account.

Encrypted locally

Your vault is encrypted in your browser with a key protected by your master password.

What VaultX stores

Everything you save — logins, passkeys, notes, cards, identities, attachments and settings — is stored in your browser's local storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Your master password is never stored or transmitted.

Website data

To offer autofill and saving, VaultX reads login forms on the pages you visit, inside your browser. That information is used only to fill or save items in your local vault; it is never sent anywhere.

Optional Google Drive sync

If — and only if — you link a Google account, VaultX uploads your already-encrypted vault to the hidden app-data folder of that account (drive.appdata scope). VaultX cannot see your other Drive files and Google cannot read the vault. Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account. You can unlink and delete the Drive copy at any time.

Permissions

VaultX asks only for what it needs: access to pages for autofill, storage for the vault, alarms for auto-lock, the clipboard for copy with auto-clear, and identity access for the optional Google sign-in.

Your control

You can export, back up or delete your data at any time from VaultX. Uninstalling the add-on removes its local data from your browser.

Changes & contact

Changes to this policy are published in this repository. Questions? Open an issue.
